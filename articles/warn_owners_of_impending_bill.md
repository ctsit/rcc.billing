# warn_owners_of_impending_bill

`warn_owners_of_impending_bill` sends an email to project owners who
have projects scheduled to be invoiced in the next month. It reminds the
owner of the impending bill and the amount of the charge. The email
provides guidance about deleting projects and reassigning ownership. It
provides links to instructions for each of these tasks. The email
describes the
[sequestration](https://ctsit.github.io/rcc.billing/articles/novel_concepts.html#sequestration)
process that ensues when an invoice is unpaid after 90 days.
