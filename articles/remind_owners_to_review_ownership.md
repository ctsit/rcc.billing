# remind_owners_to_review_ownership

`remind_owners_to_review_ownership` sends a quarterly email to every
person that owns one or more projects asking them to review the projects
they own, reassigning ownership if appropriate, or delete the project if
that is appropriate. It provides links to instructions for these
changes. The email also explains why this matters–that project owners
will be billed annually for each of their projects.
