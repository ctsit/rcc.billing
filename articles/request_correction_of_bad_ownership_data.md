# request_correction_of_bad_ownership_data

`request_correction_of_bad_ownership_data` sends one of two emails to
project designers of every project that has no owner email address. For
non-sequestered projects, the email explains projects with no valid
email address will be sequestered after 30 days. The email further
explains that projects sequestered for one year will be deleted. The
email provides a link to the project ownership page and links to
instructions for changing ownership and deleting projects. The script
sends the message a few times over the course of a month.

For sequestered projects, the email states the project was sequestered
today because it appears to be abandoned. It offers instructions to open
a service request if the sequestration is not abandoned and provides the
link to submit the service request.
