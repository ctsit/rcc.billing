# revenue_status_and_projections

`revenue_status_and_projections` is a report of financial metrics of the
project. It’s run weekly for the REDCap admins and director-level staff
and adhoc as revenue is collected. The report provides graphs and tables
on *Revenue and Receivables*, *Revenue and projections by fiscal year*,
and *Aging*
